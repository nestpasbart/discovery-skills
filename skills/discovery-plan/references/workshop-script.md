# Workshop script: intake canvas

75 minutes, 3 to 8 participants. Works on a printed poster or on a digital board the organisation allows.

## Preparation

- **Roles:** a facilitator and a timekeeper. The facilitator does not fill anything in.
- **Physical materials:** poster (A1 or A3), sticky notes in two colours (business and customers), markers, dot stickers for voting.
- **Digital materials:** an approved board set up, with a link everyone can edit.
- **Share beforehand:** the topic in one sentence, plus any research results, data or earlier insights, with their source. What was not shared beforehand does not count as evidence in the workshop.

## Agenda

| Time | Section | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Opening | Goal, and the rule: assumptions are not facts, and every assumption is welcome |
| 5–15 | 1. Burning question | 3 min writing 1 to 2 questions in silence, clustering, voting with 2 dots per person, choosing one question and naming its type |
| 15–30 | 2. Assumptions | 7 min writing in silence, at least 5 per person, one colour per column; then read out and stick up in turn, without discussion |
| 30–40 | 3. Prioritise | Place them on the 2x2 together; left (strong evidence) only with a source on the sticky note; discuss only what goes top right; choose three at most |
| 40–45 | 4. Mode | Per selected assumption: can we write down what we believe? Yes = test, no = explore |
| 45–55 | 5. Hypothesis or done criterion | Fill in the sentence in pairs per assumption; sharpen the criterion together |
| 55–65 | 6. Participants and method | Facilitator puts the method card on the table; the group chooses segment, number, source and method |
| 65–72 | 7. What we do with the outcome | Which choices can it change, who needs to hear it, when |
| 72–75 | Close | Take a photo or export; agree who reviews the plan |

## After the workshop

1. Take a photo of the poster (straight on, all sticky notes legible) or export the board as an image.
2. Attach it in your agent's chat and ask for discovery-plan. The agent turns it into `intake.md` and marks anything illegible or ambiguous with `[?]` instead of guessing.
3. Then comes `plan.md`, which the team reviews through a pull request.
